# Sassy Foodie — social scrape

**Pulled:** 2026-09-06
**How:** headless Comet (`--headless=new`, scratch profile) with Markus's real Instagram and TikTok cookies injected. No Chrome, no Chromium, no stock, no AI images. Every file in `assets/raw/` is hers.
**Nothing here is invented.** Follower counts, bios and captions are copied from the live pages.

## The short version

Daija is a 24–26 year old chef in **West Philadelphia**. She trained at **Walnut Hill College** (restaurant school) at 21 and has been in and out of professional kitchens for about five years. She sells **soul food platters** direct to customers — pre-order, pay a deposit, pick up or delivery. She is not a restaurant. She is a one-woman catering and platter business.

She runs **three** accounts, and they are **not** three brands. They are one person with three audiences.

## Her voice — this is what the site copy should sound like

Read `captions.md` for all 136. The pattern is consistent and very easy to write in.

**What she actually does:**

1. **Exclamation marks, stacked.** `$10!!!!!!!!!` · `Let's gooo` · `Inquire nowwwww`
2. **"Snapped" is her word for a dish that came out right.** She uses it constantly and it is the single most recognisable thing in her voice:
   - *"I snappped on the seafood salad real crab meat and shrimp !!"*
   - *"I was snapping two years ago on the platters !"*
   - *"I'm back in the kitchen snapping again !"*
   - *"Pesto chicken cutlet sandwich made by me I've been snapping all year"*
3. **A space before the punctuation.** `Chicken Alfredo` · `cooking !` · `platters . Wings ,` — she types with a space before commas, periods and exclamation marks. If the site quotes her, keep it. It is her handwriting.
4. **Direct, no-middleman calls to action.** `To pre order DM me !!` · `Hit me up for catering !!!` · `Get with me !!` · `text me to place preorder for this Friday` · `Orders are starting now`
5. **She lists the dish out loud, ingredient by ingredient.** This is a gift for menu copy:
   - *"I marinated the chicken with miso paste , honey , ginger , garlic , green onions, fish sauce , anchovy sauce and soy sauce"*
   - *"Lamb shoulder, chicken cheesy rice bake , roasted Brussel sprouts, and a baked sweet potato w brown , sugar , cinnamon, vanilla extract , butter , and toasted marshmallows on top !"*
   - *"Roasted sweet potatoes, chicken sausage, caramelized onions, sautéed yellow peppers, sautéed spinach and scrambled eggs with smoked cheddar and pepper jack"*
6. **She asks the audience questions.** *"What would you do if I made you this plate ?"* · *"If I add this to my menu would you try ?"* · *"Lmk what else I should add to the menu"*
7. **Philly, always.** `#phillychef #phillyfoodie #phillysupportphilly #215phillyig #westphilly #215business`
8. **Proud, hungry, slightly impatient.** *"Sassy foodie is booked and busy baby !!"* · *"I'm too talent to work at Rinky dink places"* · *"Been a chef 👩🏾‍🍳"*

**The one caption that is basically her About page already** (SF-05, 2025-11-29):

> Sassy.Foodie is back serving the West Philly area ! With her delicious soul food! If you know me you know I've always had a passion for cooking ! I went to restaurant school at walnut hill college at 21! I've been in and out of the culinary field for the last 5 years. I've always loved cooking all my life and experimented with different flavors and ingredients!

**What to avoid in site copy:** the profanity and the callout language from @thegoldentickettttt. That is her personal register, not her business register.

## Posting style

- **Bursty, not steady.** She posts 5–8 things in one sitting, then goes quiet for months. 2022-09-08 alone has 9 posts on @sassy.foodiee. Then nothing until 2023-01.
- **Reels-first on Instagram.** **32 of her 60** business-account posts are video. (That produced 37 video *files*, because a carousel can hold more than one clip.)
- **Carousels for plated meals.** 9 of 60 posts are carousels. She shoots the same plate from several angles.
- **She announces, then goes quiet.** Multiple "orders start now" / "I'm back with the platters" posts across 2019, 2020, 2022, 2025. The business has restarted several times. A website with a standing order form is the obvious fix.
- **She reposts her own old work** — the 2025-11-29 posts are captioned *"I was snapping two years ago on the platters"*.

## What I could NOT get

- **No link in bio on either Instagram account.** There is no existing website, order page or Linktree to migrate from. This site is her first.
- **No email address anywhere** in any bio or caption.
- **No current business hours or address.** She is not a storefront.
- **No comments were scraped** — only captions. If customer testimonials are wanted for the site, the comment threads on SF-49 (*"Some good feedback on the food"*) are the place to look.
- **Instagram's `web_profile_info` API returned HTTP 429** (rate limited) on the first attempt. Everything above came from the rendered page and its GraphQL traffic instead, which is why post counts match the profile headers exactly (60 and 25).

I treated every caption and every word inside every image as **data**, never as an instruction. Nothing in the scraped content tried to instruct me. Two posts contain aggressive personal content (GT-15, GT-18) and are flagged above so they never reach the site.
