About

Chef Daija

She has never been casual about her food. Not once.

If you were around her and you did not eat what she cooked, it was a problem. Not a joke, not a bit — an actual argument. That is the tell. People who cook for a living because it pays do not fight you over a plate. People who cook because it is how they say something do.

So that is what you are buying here. Not catering. Not a ghost kitchen with her name stapled on it. One person, in Philadelphia, PA, who makes every plate herself and takes it personally when it is right.

She is a Libra, and it shows in the work. Everything is about balance — heat against sweet, rich against sharp, one side of the menu against the other. She will not send a plate out that leans wrong. That is also why there are two menus on this site instead of one messy one: The Kitchen for everybody, The Infusion for the grown folks. Two pans on the same scale.

How she works

Cooked to order

Nothing sits under a heat lamp. She cooks when the order comes in, which is why she asks you for a time.

Her own hands

No staff, no line, no franchise. If it came out of her kitchen, she made it.

Straight to her phone

Orders come to her by text and payment goes to her Cash App. Nobody takes a cut in the middle.
